PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi ₹6,000 / year Direct Transfer

Financial benefit of ₹6,000 per year paid in three equal installments directly into farmer bank accounts.

🌾
PM Fasal Bima Yojana Crop Loss Insurance

Comprehensive risk insurance covering crop damage due to floods, drought, pests, and unseasonal rains.

PM Krishi Sinchai Yojana Up to 80% Drip Subsidy

Subsidy for micro-irrigation systems, farm ponds, drip sets, and solar water pumps.

Get your farm soil tested for free and receive crop-wise nutrient recommendations to improve yield.

Hassle-free credit up to ₹3 Lakhs at subsidized interest rates for seeds, fertilizers, and equipment.
