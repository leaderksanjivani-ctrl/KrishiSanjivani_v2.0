Frequently Asked Questions

🌾 How do I list my farm produce for sale? +
Go to your Dashboard and tap Sell (List Produce). Select your crop from the icon list, enter quantity & your expected price per kg, upload photos of your harvest, pin your farm location, and tap Publish.
💰 How do payments work on KrishiSanjivani? +
Buyers pay securely via Razorpay (UPI, Credit/Debit Card, NetBanking). Funds are held in escrow and released directly to the farmer's linked bank account immediately upon delivery verification.
🚚 How is delivery handled? +
We use AI route optimization to group nearby farm pickups. Buyers can select Express Delivery Now (same day) or Scheduled Delivery for a specific date and time slot.
🏛️ What is MahaDBT and how do I apply for subsidies? +
MahaDBT is Maharashtra's official Direct Benefit Transfer portal. Visit our Schemes & MahaDBT page to track subsidy applications, link your Aadhaar card, or apply for farm equipment subsidies.
🚜 Can I rent farm machinery instead of buying? +
Yes! Our Equipment Rental portal lets you rent tractors, rotavators, water pumps, and spraying drones by the day from verified local owners.
