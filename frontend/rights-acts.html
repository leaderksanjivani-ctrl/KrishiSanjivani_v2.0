📜 Know Your Rights

Farmer Rights & Legal Protections

Simplified legal guidance in plain language — understand your MSP rights, direct sale freedoms, and land protections without complex jargon.

⚖️

MSP Guarantee Rights

Minimum Support Price Protections

The government sets MSP for 22 mandated crops. If market rates drop below MSP during harvest season, government agencies are obligated to purchase at MSP rates.

🤝

Direct Sale Freedom (APMC Reform)

Right to Sell Anywhere

Farmers are legally free to sell their produce directly to consumers, bulk buyers, or via digital platforms like KrishiSanjivani without paying mandatory middleman commissions at local APMC mandis.

📋

Contract Farming Act

Written Price Agreement Protections

Under contract farming regulations, written contracts must specify pre-agreed purchase prices. Buyers cannot refuse produce or reduce prices after harvest if quality standards are met.

🏦

Debt Relief & Money Lending Act

Protection Against Illegal Lenders

Unlicensed money lenders charging exorbitant interest rates are illegal. Farmers have the right to restructure institutional farm loans under state debt relief schemes.
